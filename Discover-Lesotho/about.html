WELCOME TO THE MOUNTAIN KINGDOM

Discover the Heart of Lesotho

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa, completely surrounded by South Africa. Known as the Mountain Kingdom, it is celebrated for its dramatic highlands, distinctive landscapes and rich Basotho heritage.

Its identity is shaped by traditional clothing, music, crafts, village life and a strong sense of community. These traditions make exploring Lesotho a meaningful cultural experience.

Discover Lesotho brings together information about destinations, nature, outdoor adventures and cultural experiences to help visitors explore the country.
